Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Simple Sudoku ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Summary

Simple Sudoku has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of our own. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information. Your game data stays on your device. The app does display ads, and our advertising provider collects certain device data — that is described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on your device so it can remember your progress between sessions. This information is never sent to us and never leaves your device:

  • Your selected theme and colour palette
  • Your preferred game mode and difficulty
  • Whether sound effects are enabled
  • Your in-progress puzzle, so an unfinished game can be resumed
  • Your level for each game mode
  • Your daily win streak count and the date of your last win

This data is held in the operating system's standard app-preferences storage. Deleting or uninstalling the app removes all of it. We have no ability to access, recover, or restore it.

Advertising

The app shows interstitial ads supplied by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information including:

  • Device advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's Identifier for Advertisers)
  • IP address and approximate, IP-derived location
  • Device and operating-system characteristics
  • Interactions with ads shown in the app, such as views and taps

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own policies. We do not receive any of this information in a form that identifies you; we can only see aggregate, anonymous performance totals. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your advertising choices

  • iOS: control app tracking under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, under Settings › Google › Ads.

Opting out does not remove ads from the app; it makes them less personalised.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not build user profiles, and we do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not require an account or any form of registration.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data — including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict its processing, and to object to processing or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, requests of this kind generally need to be directed to Google as the advertising provider. You can exercise full control over the data held on your device by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. If you have questions, contact us at the address below.

Links to other services

Tapping an ad, or a link within the app, may open a website or app operated by a third party. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those destinations, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be reflected in an app update. Continuing to use the app after a change takes effect means you accept the revised policy.
